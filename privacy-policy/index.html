1. Introduction

Durbar ("the App", "we", "us") is a college and school companion app for students and professors. It provides posts, short videos, notes, mock tests, a calendar, student directories and profile features. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, why, how it is shared, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: email address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication and never stored by us in plain text), or your Google account email if you use "Continue with Google".
  • Profile details: full name, role (student, professor, captain or moderator), gender, phone number, date of birth, class level, section, assigned subject (for professors) and profile photo.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, comments (including comment images), likes, short videos, notes, PDF and document attachments, links (including YouTube links), and mock test questions and sets.
  • Invite and access tokens: student or professor tokens you enter to verify your role.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage and diagnostics data through Firebase Analytics, such as app opens, screens viewed, device model, OS version and app version.
  • Activity counters such as likes and short video view counts.
  • Timestamps for account creation, posts, comments and uploads.

2.3 Information stored only on your device

  • Login session information and credentials protected with Android encrypted storage.
  • Mock test progress and results kept locally.
  • App preferences and notification state.
Biometric or screen-lock confirmation (used for sensitive actions such as deleting your account) is performed entirely by Android. Durbar never receives, sees or stores your fingerprint, face data or PIN.

2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers, and we do not show third-party ads.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your role.
  • To display your profile, posts, comments, shorts, notes and other content to other users as described in section 4.
  • To show posts and content relevant to your class and section.
  • To send in-app notifications about new posts, notes, messages and calendar events.
  • To show the Nepali BS calendar, holidays and events.
  • To deliver app updates and announcements.
  • To maintain, debug and improve the App and keep it safe from abuse.
  • To respond to your support requests and enforce our Terms of Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Who can see your information

  • Public to other Durbar users: your name, profile photo, role and badge, posts, comments, likes and shorts. Posts set to "Everyone" are visible to all users; posts set to "Section" are intended for members of your section.
  • Visible to other signed-in users in the student directory or profile pages: details such as class, section and, where the feature is used, your phone number so that others can contact you.
  • Shared links: if you share a post, short, photo, PDF or profile link, anyone with the link may be able to open that content.
  • Administrators and moderators: app administrators can manage content, roles and updates and may view content stored in the App's database to keep the community safe.

Please do not post information you want to keep private.

7. Background notifications

Durbar does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging. Instead, a foreground service keeps a connection to our Firebase database to detect new posts, notes and messages and show you a notification. This service shows a persistent notification while it runs. It may use a small amount of battery and mobile data. You can disable notifications in Android settings at any time, though you may then miss new activity.

8. Security

We use Firebase Authentication, HTTPS-only network traffic, Android encrypted storage for locally saved credentials, and database rules to protect your data. We do not store your password in plain text on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Choose a strong, unique password and keep your device locked.

9. Data retention and account deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account from Account Center → Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm with your password and with your device's screen lock or biometrics.

Deleting your account removes your profile record from our database and deletes your login from Firebase Authentication. Please note:

  • Content you posted (posts, comments, shorts, notes) or files stored with Cloudinary and ImgBB may not be removed automatically. To request removal of specific content, contact us using the details below.
  • Backups and logs held by third-party providers may persist for a limited time under their own policies.
  • We may keep information where required for security, legal compliance or dispute resolution.

10. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: view and edit your profile details in the App.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the App or by contacting us.
  • Content removal: ask us to remove content you uploaded.
  • Notifications: manage through Android settings.
  • Permissions: revoke permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • Backups: the App allows Android backup of app data. You can turn off device backups in your Android settings.

To exercise any right, email us at the address in section 13.

11. Age and minors

Durbar is intended for students and teachers at the higher-secondary level and above. You must be at least 13 years old to use it. Users under 18 should use the App with the awareness of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Effective" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy. For significant changes we will try to notify you in the App.
